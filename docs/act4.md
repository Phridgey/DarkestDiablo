# Act IV plan (decided 2026-11-21, not built yet)

Act IV under the flattening plan (`docs/flattening.md`): a short act, about levels 54 to 72,
highest rune Ohm, camp-wide progress, re-runs until a hero is more than 2 levels over a stop,
forks with no pick limit. Every map is a copy with no stairs (reached by the route map, left by
Town Portal).

| Tier | Stop | Kind | Target level | Cleared when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plains of Despair (Izual) | forced, XP | 56-58 | Izual dies |
| 2 | Abaddon (Act V red portal) | fork: XP | 61-64 | (XP grind) |
| 2 | Pit of Acheron (Act V red portal) | fork: MF | 61-64 | (MF run) |
| 2 | Infernal Pit (Act V red portal) | fork: crafting | 61-64 | (crafting hunt) |
| 3 | Hellforge, cut down | forced, crafting | 66-68 | Mephisto's Soulstone is smashed on the forge |
| 4 | Chaos Sanctuary, full length | forced, XP + MF | 70-72 | Diablo dies |

- **Plains of Despair** is an XP stop (+40% experience, triple gold). Izual's quest reward is
  tripled like every once-only reward: 6 skill points instead of 2 (Tyrael's 2, plus 4 from the
  plugin on the camp's first clear).
- **The three red portals** are the Act V red-portal maps, borrowed by Act IV: one XP, one MF,
  one crafting (which map gets which kind is a first choice and can be swapped). Each is linear
  and about twice the size of the usual arena maps.
- **Hellforge** is cut down to a short path to the forge, with Hephasto at it. Its monsters use
  the crafting-hunt copies. The forge's reward is tripled: three runes, each Pul or higher (an
  exception to the Ohm cap). Ber, Sur, Jah, Cham and Zod are 5% each; the other 75% is shared
  evenly by Pul, Um, Mal, Ist, Gul, Vex, Ohm and Lo (about 9.4% each).
- **Chaos Sanctuary** keeps its full map: the five seals, their three seal bosses, then Diablo.
  It is both an XP and an MF stop: monster copies worth 40% more experience, triple gold, and
  magic-find loot. Diablo's death clears it and the act.
- No River of Flame: the route map takes the hero straight to the Hellforge, then to the
  sanctuary.

# Act III plan (decided 2026-11-21, not built yet)

Act III under the flattening plan (`docs/flattening.md`): about levels 36 to 54, highest rune Lem,
camp-wide progress, re-runs until a hero is more than 2 levels over a stop, forks with no pick
limit. Every map is a copy with no stairs (reached by the route map, left by Town Portal), and
every map is linear: one way forward, nowhere to get lost.

| Tier | Stop | Kind | Target level | Cleared when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Spider Cavern, one room | forced, XP | 38-42 | Khalim's Eye is picked up |
| 1 | Flayer Dungeon, one room | forced, XP | 38-42 | Khalim's Brain is picked up |
| 1 | Kurast Sewers, one room | forced, XP | 38-42 | Khalim's Heart is picked up |
| 2 | Ruined Temple (Lam Esen's Tome) | fork: XP | 45-48 | Lam Esen's Tome is picked up |
| 2 | Swampy Pit, one room, waves | fork: MF | 45-48 | (MF run: as the Act I MF runs) |
| 2 | Lower Kurast, every chest a super chest | fork: crafting | 45-48 | (crafting hunt) |
| 3 | Travincal | forced, MF loot | 50-51 | the Compelling Orb is smashed |
| 4 | Durance of Hate 3, Mephisto | forced | 53-54 | Mephisto dies |

- **Tier 1 is three forced stops on one row**, like Act II's tier 3: any order, any heroes; once
  all three are cleared by heroes of the camp, tier 2 opens. Each is a short straight strip of
  swamp ending in the one room that holds Khalim's part (the swamp is the way in, not a stop of
  its own). They are XP stops (+40% experience, triple gold), to keep the hero on the act's
  level curve.
- **The Jade Figurine:** any unique monster in the tier 1 stops may drop it (Meshif's quest,
  the Golden Bird). Its reward, Alkor's potion of life, is tripled like every once-only reward:
  +60 life instead of +20 (the potion gives 20, the plugin adds 40 on the hero's first use).
- **Lam Esen's Tome** gives 15 stat points instead of 5: reading it gives the usual 5, and the
  plugin adds 10 on the camp's first clear (as Radament's book).
- **Lower Kurast** is cut down to a straight path, and every chest on it is a super chest (the
  sparkling ones). It is the crafting-materials run.
- **Travincal** uses the MF-run monster copies (magic-find loot, as Ancient Tunnels). The High
  Council (three members) drops Khalim's Flail as usual.
- **One hero crafts Khalim's Will:** the camp clears the three tier 1 stops with any heroes, but
  the hero who smashes the orb must hold all three parts and the Flail (quest items cannot go in
  the shared stash). A hero missing a part re-runs that stop for it (each tier 1 stop gives its
  part again on every run).
- **Hratli's socket:** once a hero holds all three of Khalim's parts (before making Khalim's Will),
  Hratli gives a socket reward (`docs/flattening.md`, "Socket rewards").
- **Khalim's Will is kept:** the hero cubes the Flail with the Eye, Brain and Heart into Khalim's
  Will and smashes the Compelling Orb; that clears Travincal and opens Durance 3 on the route map.
- **Durance 3:** the hero arrives near Mephisto's bridge; his death clears the stop and the act.
